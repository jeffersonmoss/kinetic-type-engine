# Deterministic engine: kit

The deterministic engine builds a kinetic-type lyric video for one song as a 3D scene: every sung word is a block of type on a plaque, plaques attach to each other along one trail, and a planned camera flies the trail so each word is read when it is sung. Every choice comes from a seeded random stream (the seed is the song title), so the same input always gives the same scene. Each section is built 200 times with different salts, every candidate is checked against the rulebook, and the best passing one (or, when none passes, the least bad one) is kept. The sections are then joined into one whole-song scene, checked again as a whole, and rendered frame by frame in headless Chrome.

It was built for Room for One (LeBron Glaze) between 21 and 22 November 2026 in the prototype repo `~/Experiments/Room for One Music Video/prototype/Deterministic engine - h1 - word transitions 22 November/` (branch `word-transitions`, commit 4e2d2b0) and moved here, song-agnostic, on 22 November 2026. It lives beside the Remotion engine (`../engine`, `../KIT.md`) and shares nothing with it except the font metrics in `../fonts/`.

## 1. What is in this folder

1. Planning, in pipeline order: `plan_words.mjs` (words into clusters and shots, emphasis), `style.mjs` (palette styles per word), `compose_plaques.mjs` (one fitted plaque per shot), `plan_trail.mjs` (folds: how each plaque attaches to the last), `camera.mjs` (camera keys and visibility), `plan_joints.mjs` (attached word transitions: the entrance of each word), `primitives.mjs` (the entrance primitives), `rng.mjs` (seeded streams), `interp.js` (camera and word interpolation, shared with the viewer).
2. Checks: `checks.mjs` (per-section checks and score), `shown_gate.mjs` (the focused word is shown whole, with camera repair), `whole_song_checks.mjs` (sync and idleness on the joined scene), `entrance_counts.mjs` (pop share), `scene_probe.mjs`.
3. Builders: `build_best.mjs` (one section, best of N), `assemble_sections.mjs` (join sections, hide gone plaques, the reveal), `build_song.mjs` (the whole song from a song's input folder).
4. Post-passes: `apply_notes.mjs` (the song's colour rules, reveal copies, sung and beat pulses through `sung_pulses.mjs`), `run_elements.mjs` with `elements.mjs` (picture elements such as Room for One's hand and rings).
5. Output: `capture.mjs` and `viewer.html` (render), `click_track.mjs` (ear check of repeats and ad-libs), `provenance.mjs` and `debug_svg.mjs` (per-section reports and plaque sheets).
6. `new_song_input.mjs`: drafts a new song's input folder.
7. `palettes/`: `room for one.json` (the original) and `starter - two team colours.json` (template for a new song).
8. `rulebook/`: the rules (section 5).

Font metrics: `../fonts/room-for-one-variety.metrics.json` (fonts loaded by `viewer.html` from Google Fonts: Anton, Graduate, Barlow Condensed, Bebas Neue, League Gothic, Big Shoulders Display, Archivo Black, Saira Stencil One, Teko). `KTE_METRICS=<file>` overrides it.

Node 20 or later. No npm packages: the engine uses only Node built-ins. Rendering needs Google Chrome at `/Applications/Google Chrome.app` and ffmpeg.

## 2. The per-song input folder

Each song keeps its input in its own workspace, in `<song workspace>/engine input/`. Paths inside are relative to that folder.

9. `song settings.json` (required):
   9.1. `title`: seeds every random choice. Changing it changes every build.
   9.2. `audio`: the song mp3 (capture.mjs muxes it; the click track is made from it).
   9.3. `map`: the song map (`Song map.json`, from the kinetic-type-video skill's `scripts/build_song_map.py`): `duration_s`, `lines` [{id, text, start_s, end_s, words}], `words` [{text, start_s, end_s, line}], `beats` [{t, index, bar, beat_in_bar, energy}], and the rest of the skill's map.
   9.4. `palette`: palette file (section 2, item 13).
   9.5. `sections` [{n, name, lines: [first, last], seam?}]: the sections built one by one, cut at the song's own phrase boundaries. `seam: "cut"` makes the join into that section a hard cut.
   9.6. Optional: `fast_words` (keyword mode, ON by default; section "Keyword mode" below), `ending` ({reveal: true|false, hold: "<word>" | "last", min_hold_s: 0.5}; section "Endings" below), `timing_corrections`, `sung_repeats`, `song_notes`, `picture_elements`, `vocal_stem` (snaps the click track to vocal onsets), `opening` ({label, scene, words, chosen}: an approved scene used as is instead of building the first lines), `display_numbers` ({"twenty-four": "24"}: number words shown as digits), `fps` (30), `seam_gap` (gap at the joins, as a share of the previous plaque's height; default 0.3), `read_tail_s` (0.5), `reveal_pull_s` (4), `reveal_hold_s` (3).
10. `timing corrections.json`: `words` [{index, text, start_s, end_s}] (index into the map's words; text must match or the build stops) and `lines` [{id, start_s?, end_s?}].
11. `sung repeats.json`: `events` [{text, t, line, source, display?}]: chants, echoes and ad-libs the lyric map lacks. They become pulses on words already on screen, or echo words, and the click track.
12. `song notes.mjs` (optional): `export function applySongNotes(scene, log)`, run on the joined scene before the generic post-pass. Room for One's holds its colour rules (team colours only on the rings won with each team, 76ers stripes on KING!, the last RING! and 24) and its hook 1 chant pulses.
13. `palette.json`: `ground`, `panel`, `panel_side`, `styles` [{id, kind: block|fill|outline, colours: {background?, text}, role: hero|accent|plain|quiet, group, flat_fill?, outline_twin?, on_panel?}], `voices.adlib`, `pins` ({word id or text: style id}). Every text colour needs 3:1 contrast on its background, panel and ground (`style.mjs` checkPalette throws otherwise). Style ids that `viewer.html` does not know are drawn from these colours.
14. `picture elements.mjs` (optional): `export function plan(scene, { findWord })` returning the plan `elements.mjs` applies ({artDir, holdRevealUntil, elements, sets, timing}). See Room for One's for the hand and rings.

Room for One's input is the worked example: `~/Experiments/Room for One Music Video/engine input/`.

## Keyword mode for fast words (22 November 2026)

- On by default for every song (Jefferson: "If it's a problem, it's a problem"). Where the lyric is sung too fast to read, only the important words are placed; the rest are left out of the scene, the sync check and the sung pulses, and listed in the provenance file ("Keyword mode") with every stretch's start, end and peak rate.
- Turn it off per song with `"fast_words": {"mode": "all_words"}` (or `"enabled": false`), or for one build with `build_song.mjs --all-words`. Off, the build is identical to a build without the feature.
- Detection (`keywords.mjs`): a word's rate is the number of words starting within `window_s` (2.0 s) centred on it, divided by `window_s`. A stretch starts at the first word over `rate_on` (4.5 words per second) and runs while the rate stays at or above `rate_off` (3.5). Stretches shorter than `min_stretch_s` (1.5 s) are ignored.
- Kept: names and nicknames (written in capitals, or capitalised mid-sentence somewhere in the song, plus team wordmark names, display numbers and palette pins), numbers, the rhyme word at each line end, other content words, and `keep`. Dropped: function words (articles, pronouns, auxiliaries, prepositions, conjunctions and their contractions; `keywords.mjs` FUNCTION_WORDS) plus `drop`. Kept words start at least `min_gap_s` (0.40 s) apart; in a clash the higher rank stays (name or number, then rhyme word, then the rest; ties keep the earlier). Every line keeps at least one word. An approved opening scene is never thinned.
- Overrides: `rate_on`, `rate_off`, `window_s`, `min_stretch_s`, `min_gap_s`, `keep` [words], `drop` [words].
- Unit check: `node --test test_keywords.mjs`.

## Endings, readable size and composition (engine fix brief 3, 22 November 2026)

- `ending.reveal: false` switches the final pull-back off: the song ends on `ending.hold` (a word's text, its last sung occurrence, or `"last"`), held still from the moment it comes to rest to the last frame. Big Toothy Grins ends on its last WOLVES wordmark. With the reveal on (the default; Room for One), the reveal starts early enough to land and hold still for `ending.min_hold_s` (0.5 s) before the end, and the pull-back stops at the `--reveal-coverage` floor, centred on the layout, instead of shrinking the whole layout to a strand.
- Never end mid-move: the build fails (exit 1) when the camera moves in the last `min_hold_s` or a shown word is moving on the last frame.
- Readable size (reported and ranked, not a hard gate): a sung word's letters should reach 10% of the frame height by onset + 3 frames. A word under that, which only grows once the camera arrives (1.5 times its onset size within a second) or is under 4% outright, is a speck. The repair lands the arriving move earlier or makes it shorter, else reframes the hold; each change is kept only when it lowers the cost (partly shown 1000, dropped 10, speck or sparse 5). Riders that are small by design in their own framing are not specks.
- Sparse frame: two consecutive words both under 15% letter height (12% before brief 4), more than a third of the frame apart, with no anchor word (20% or more, was 15%) whole in view and readable: a clipped, mirrored, upside-down or back-facing word is never an anchor. Specks and sparse frames rank section candidates right after the shown-whole gate (`13.1.20_readable_size_and_sparse_frames`); they do not by themselves fail a candidate.
- Line groups retire together: when the overlap repair retires a word, the other words of its plaque that are past their reading moment go at the same frame, so a punchline never leaves before its neighbours.
- Lone focus words (`composition.mjs` tightenLone): a sung word alone in its hold, with no other word whole in frame, is centred and brought in until its letters are 22% of the frame height (within 80% of the frame width), when the gates and the local overlap count stay as good.
- Edge clutter (`hideEdgeClutter`, revised by brief 4): during a camera hold, a word outside its focus window (its appearance through onset + 15 frames) whose letters are not at least 95% in frame is hidden from the end of its focus window (or the hold start) to the hold end, and on the last frames of a camera move of 8 frames or fewer into the hold on which it is already cut. A camera cut after a word's onset ends its focus window. The share is an 11 x 5 grid that includes the edges, over the letter band, or over the whole box for a word drawn on its own background block, so a halved word, a sliver of two letters or the foot of a block always counts. The hide also runs into the first frames of a camera move of 8 frames or fewer out of the hold while the word is still cut (only while another word is whole in frame).
- Wordmark images are resampled once, at high quality. A 710 px wide source is drawn at about 1.7 times its size in the word texture, so its edges soften at 720p; a sharper wordmark needs a source at least about 1,300 px wide, or an SVG.

## Moving openings and team wordmarks

- `intro` in `song settings.json` is a list of `{image, hold_s, dissolve_s?, dissolve_at_s?, exit?}`. Team logos used here are the team's real logos (Type rule 4). Image paths are relative to the song input. The first starts on frame zero. Each next image begins its dissolve after the preceding minimum hold. Set `dissolve_at_s` to delay that dissolve to an absolute song time. The last image ends at vocal onset. Give the last image a positive hold and omit its dissolve. All images share one motion path: a steady push (`intro_push_per_s`, default 0.045, about 21% scale change over a 4.4 s hold) and a linear drift (`intro_drift`, default 0.06 of the frame width), with no easing or wobble. Each PNG is sized and centred on its opaque content (alpha bounds, `png_alpha.mjs`), not its canvas, so a badge with a transparent margin lands exactly over the previous badge during a dissolve and the motion carries on through it. The last image cuts at the first vocal by default. Set `exit: "slide"` for a lateral exit. PNG, JPEG, WebP and SVG assets are embedded in the final scene. With a pre-vocal gap and no images, the song title fills it. Up to one second of opening blank is allowed to accommodate a short pickup.
- `team_wordmarks` maps individual team names and nicknames to `{image?, font?, colours}`. Matching ignores case and surrounding punctuation, and covers lyric words, sung echoes and reveal copies.
  - `image` (preferred when the team's lettering exists only as artwork): a local PNG, JPEG, WebP or SVG, the team's real wordmark (Type rule 4) on a transparent background. The word is drawn as that image, aspect ratio kept, fitted inside the planned word box (94% of its width, 90% of its height) where the lettering would be. Colours: `tint` recolours the image through its alpha (omit it to keep the artwork's own colours); `stroke` draws an outline around the letters' silhouette and `accent` a second, outer outline; `background` fills the word's block. Big Toothy Grins' WOLVES uses the navy jersey lettering with a white stroke, an aurora green accent and a lake blue block.
  - `font`: a local TTF, OTF, WOFF or WOFF2, used when no image is given. Colours may specify `text`, `stroke` and `background`. The viewer fits supplied faces to the planned word box.
  - Neither: the existing lettering is kept with the supplied colours. The provenance file lists every entry as IMAGE, FONT or NEITHER, then a one-line summary of which team words used which.
- The final joined scene is repaired for both whole-scene rules together (`overlap_gate.mjs` `repairShownAndOverlap`): the shown-whole repair and the overlap repair alternate until neither changes anything, so a camera repair cannot leave an overlap and an overlap retirement cannot leave a focused word partly shown. The shown-whole repair can also reframe the opening hold (before the first camera key) and delay a short camera move whole when the focused word would otherwise leave the frame too soon.
- Section selection ranks candidates in tiers: focused words whole and no projected overlap, then focused words whole, then the rest by fewest partly shown words. Overlap never outranks the shown-whole rule, because the joined repair can still clear overlaps. Each candidate's shown-whole result is re-judged after its own overlap repair.
- The projected overlap gate scans the lyric sequence before the pullback. It uses clipped word texture rectangles as a conservative ink envelope and checks projected panel obstruction. It may retire old words and plaques. The selector uses overlap-free variants whenever any exist. Remaining overlaps fail the whole-song build and are listed with times and words. Candidate check frequencies count how many candidates failed each check.

## Readable orientation and no long empty stretches (engine fix brief 4, 22 November 2026)

- No mirrored text, ever (`hideUnreadable`, `shown_gate.mjs` readableOrientation): on every frame, a word outside its focus window that is in frame and seen from behind (its face points away from the camera) or mirrored (its projected right and up axes swap handedness) is hidden for that frame. One tilted more than 100 degrees from upright is hidden too while the camera is still; during a camera move a turning word may briefly pass the vertical. The build reports how many words were hidden and how many focused words were seen unreadably (those are left alone, since hiding them would break the shown-whole gate).
- Something on screen (`fillEmptyFrames`, gate in `build_song.mjs`; Jefferson, 22 November 2026: "there shouldn't be any gaps longer than a quarter second of dead frame, and neither the first nor last frame should be black"). Over the whole song, from frame 0 to the last frame: no empty stretch longer than the song setting `empty_gap_max_s` (default 0.25 s: 7 frames at 30 fps, so 8 or more empty frames in a row fail), and the first and last frames are never empty. Empty stretches of 7 frames or fewer are fine: counted in the report, never filled, never a failure or a re-pick. A frame is empty when no intro overlay (badge, title card) is up, no picture element and no word is at least half in frame and readable. A stretch over the limit is filled by, in order: (1) keeping the outgoing words on through it, (2) switching the next words (at rest, within 4 frames) on earlier, (3) landing a camera move that hides it at its start, (4) holding the camera on its last filled pose (`key.hold` in `interp.js`, which the render also uses) with those words kept on, or (5, brief 5) skipping a camera move that lands on an empty hold in which no word is sung, so the camera stays on its last filled pose until the next move and those words stay on; a try is kept when fewer frames nearby are empty and neither overlaps nor partly shown focused words increase. What no try fills fails the build (exit 1), and no section pick, fallback or other setting waives it. The check runs after the song appearance pass, so intro overlays count.
- Echoes never retire a sung word: when an echo floats over its own held word, the held word steps back only while the echo shows; an echo that would cover any other word is cut short or dropped. (Room for One's outro chant had been black for 16 s in every engine build, because the overlap repair retired RING!, which carries nine re-sung pulses, for its first echo.)
- Rider floor (`plan_words.mjs` RIDER_FLOOR 0.20, was 0.13): a rider's nominal letter size is at least a fifth of the shot's biggest anchor, so riders sung before their anchor arrives are not specks or lone small words.

## Size, colours, outlines and bold jersey fonts (engine fix brief 5, 22 November 2026)

- Big enough or not shown (`composition.mjs` enforceMinSize; Jefferson: "We should either show a word big enough to be seen or not show it at all"). Runs on the joined scene after the lone-word pass. For each camera hold a word is in, its letters are measured at their largest (cap height as a share of the frame height, frames where it is at rest, at least 95% in frame and readable).
  - Minimum: song setting `min_word_h` (default 0.09, Type rule 1 as corrected in brief 5 change 5) when the frame is crowded; `room_factor` (default 1.5, so 13.5%) times that when it is not.
  - Crowded: at that frame at least `crowded_words` (default 4, was 3 until brief 6) words, this one included, are at least half in frame, or their projected boxes together cover at least `crowded_area` (default 0.30) of the frame.
  - A word under its minimum is grown to it (box, cap, font size and padding together, at most 2.5 times), about its centre or, failing that, away from a neighbour (about an edge midpoint or corner of its box). A growth is kept only when it adds no projected overlap over the word's life and leaves it no less shown at its onset.
  - What cannot grow: in a hold that is its focus window, the word is dropped for its whole life (`dropped: "size"`); in a later hold it is hidden for that hold while another word of at least `min_word_h` stays on screen. Echoes, reveal copies and wordmarks are not resized; a word on a hinge is measured and may be dropped or hidden but is never grown; a word entering past a clip plane (tick, slide-out from behind its host) is grown with its slide scaled by the same factor, and only if it still starts wholly behind the plane and is wholly past it when the clip ends (rough cut 6b: ARE at 34 s had been left at 8.7%). Only non-important words are dropped (Type rule 2): an important word (keyword mode rank 2 or 3: a name, number, player name or the rhyme word at a line end) that cannot grow stays and is reported. The pass runs after the clutter and unreadable hides and repeats (up to 4 passes) until nothing changes, since a drop can leave a neighbour's frame uncrowded; the clutter and unreadable hides then run once more. A growth's overlap is counted with every size drop shown.
  - The empty-screen rule outranks it: the fills are tried on a copy, and for each stretch they cannot clear one drop or hide inside it (the one covering most of it) is undone, the word shown as it was; this repeats (up to 12 rounds) and every undo is listed in the report.
- Dropped words are allowed ("Every word does not have to be seen for the video to pass"). A word dropped for size is exempt from the shown-whole gates (`sungWords`), is listed by the sync check as dropped (allowed, never late), and does not count as visible activity in the idle check. The partly-shown gate still holds for every word that is shown.
- Text colours (`style.mjs` normalizePalette, run by build_best on the song palette): text is cream `#F3EBDD` or white `#FFFFFF`, or a flat grey, 75% `#BFBFBF` or 50% `#808080`, for words meant to be quiet. Any other non-team text or on-panel colour snaps to the nearest of these (Big Toothy Grins and Room for One: grey `#7d7a76` and its panel `#96938e` become `#808080`). Team colours stay on team styles: a block style, a style sharing a block's group, a block's chromatic flat fill, or one marked `team: true`.
- No outlines (`composition.mjs` noOutlines; brief 5 change 2, Jefferson: "Let's keep all letters solid color and just not do outlines"). Every word is drawn solid, team words included: an outline style becomes its flat twin in the same colour, any white edge is removed, and team wordmarks lose their stroke and accent rings. A team word's second team colour comes from its background block in the other team colour. The grey style no longer carries a built-in white outline in `viewer.html`. The 76ers striped jersey words (Room for One) keep their per-letter thickening, which is in each letter's own colour. Rule wording for this awaits Jefferson's approval; this note only describes the behaviour.
- Jersey font for player names and numbers: numerals already take the jersey font; words listed in song setting `player_names` (possessive 's ignored) take it too. Team names use their `team_wordmarks` image, the team's real wordmark (Type rule 4).
- Bold jersey fonts (`style.mjs` JERSEY_FONTS, today `collegiate`, Graduate). Graduate has a single weight (Google Fonts serves only 400; a request for 700 fails), so its thin letters come from the font, not from our weight choice, and it is bolded by offset: drawn with an outward stroke in the letter's own colour, song setting `jersey_bold` (default 0.10) times the cap height wide, so half of it (5% of the cap) lies outside each edge; mitred (limit 2) so corners stay square. `compose_plaques.mjs` wordBox adds the same width to the box, so layout and the overlap checks see the drawn letters; the viewer reads `meta.jersey_bold`. The 76ers striped jersey words keep their own thickening.
- The projected overlap gate judges the final scene as written, after the size, outline and fill passes (brief 5); before, it reported the joint repair's count.
- Type rules check (`type_rules_check.mjs`, read-only): `node type_rules_check.mjs "<song>/engine input" "<final scene>" [--out <report.md>] [--player-names A,B] [--team-names A,B]` lists every failure of the Type rules below by section, with the time range, the measured values and what a rebuild of that section would change.

## Type rules

Approved by Jefferson, 22 November 2026 (brief 5 change 3, numbers corrected by change 5).

1. Letter height is at least 13.5% of the frame height, or 9% where space is crowded. A word that won't fit at that size is dropped.
2. Keywords are never dropped except by necessity, at the model's discretion. Other words are dropped only when space or pace requires it, fillers first. A dropped word is reported, not failed.
3. Letters are one solid colour, with no outlines: cream, white, flat grey at 75% or 50%, or a team colour. Keywords (a subject, main verb, object, name, or a word stressed on the beat) are never grey. A second team colour goes on the word's background block when a background or strong team identification is needed; a team colour with no background is an acceptable creative choice. Every numeral is judged as a score or a jersey number. Scores are drawn as scoreboard bulb digits: lit bulbs orange-red, unlit bulbs dim dark grey. Jersey numbers follow rule 4.
4. Player names and jersey numbers use a bold jersey font and are never grey: white letters on the darker team colour, or letters in team colours. A name used in another sense is not a name. Team names and logos use the team's real wordmark or logo.
- Motion blur samples (`viewer.html` sampleCount, brief 6): samples are at most 3 px apart (up to 32 per frame), and under 4 px of motion a frame is drawn sharp. At 10 px spacing two half-strength copies 13 px apart read as a drop shadow (SHOOTING at 101.6 s in Big Toothy Grins rough cut 6). Renders with long fast moves take longer.
- The Type rules check counts as outlined any word with a stroke, a white edge or an outline style, and, in a scene built before brief 5 (no `meta.jersey_bold`), any word in the grey style, which that viewer drew with a white outline.

## No word static and partly on screen (engine fix brief 6 item 5, 22 November 2026)

Jefferson: a word's boundary has only three allowed states: moving onto the screen, moving off it, or wholly on screen. Already-shown words may still be partly visible at the edges while the camera or the word is moving.

- Visible share: the word's projected ink (letter band, or the whole box for block words) clipped exactly to the frame, divided by its full projected area. A word facing away is skipped.
- Partly on screen: visible share above 0.005 and below 0.995 (`PARTIAL_LO`, `PARTIAL_HI`), on a frame where the word is drawn.
- Static: the visible share changes by less than 0.01 since the frame before (`STATIC_RATE`) for 3 frames or more in a row (`STATIC_FRAMES`; brief 7 lowered it from 4 and dropped the centred difference, which missed a camera pausing 3 or 4 frames mid-sweep). These numbers are best guesses.
- Brief 7: the visible share also counts clipping by the word's clip planes and cover by other plaques' panel occluders (a ray test from the camera), and the letter band includes the drawn ink outside the cap height (3% overshoot, plus half the bold stroke each side on jersey letters).
- Resolution (`hideStaticPartials` in `composition.mjs`): the word is hidden for its whole partly-on-screen stretch (opacity 0, restored after). A focused word must still be whole from where it comes to rest through its onset and for `SHOWN_FRAMES`; it is hidden only from the end of that requirement. If the static frames fall inside the requirement, it is left and the gate fails. It runs after the size pass, and again after the empty-screen fills.
- Gate: any static partly-on-screen run left in the final scene fails the join (`STATIC PARTLY ON SCREEN FAIL`, exit 1). The provenance report lists every word hidden.
- Before hiding, the word is slid along or across its plaque (0.25 to 1.5 of its width or height) when that makes it whole with no new overlap (counted in overlap frames, with size drops shown) and no new shown-whole failure. A non-important word that cannot be slid is dropped (`dropped: 'static'`).
- Not yet tried: moving a word fully in or pushing it fully out with the camera. Hiding was enough for every case so far.

## Engine fix brief 7 (22 November 2026)

- **A failing gate stops the join.** The join works in memory; `Whole song - final - scene.json` is written only when every gate passes. On a failure the scene goes to `Whole song - FAILED GATES - not for render - scene.json`, an earlier final scene in the folder is renamed `Whole song - earlier final, before a failed join - scene.json`, the provenance report says "GATES FAILED: not for render", and the build exits 1.
- **Undersized-only frames are empty.** A frame whose only readable words are under that frame's size minimum (Type rule 1, x0.97 slack) counts as empty for the 0.25 s rule. Fill 6, the swell (`swellSmallWords`): for an empty stretch the other fills leave, the small words on screen swell to the minimum (a scale segment up over 3 frames, back down by the end of the stretch or as the next word arrives, at most x3.5), trying subsets of the words (fewest and keywords first), pivots on a 3 by 3 grid over the group and each word, and earlier ends. A swell is kept only if no long stretch is left and it adds no overlap frames, partly shown focused word or static partial. Words stay at their own size outside the swell, so the Type rules check still lists them.
- **Zoom entrances** (`floorZoomEntrances`) start at no less than half the size minimum with room (`ZOOM_FLOOR` 0.5); kept only with no new overlap frames.
- **Edge-on words** (`hideUnreadable`): a word whose face is within `EDGE_ON_DEG` (20°) of the line to the camera counts as unreadable while the camera is still, so outside its focus window it is hidden.
- **Overlap at the drawn size.** The overlap scan includes the viewer's draw-time scale: the stamp landing (1.1 to 1 over 3 frames) and the ripple throw at each sung pulse (up to 1.18). `tamePulses` lowers a colliding pulse to 1.12, then 1.06, then drops it, and a colliding stamp to 1.05, then off (`w.pulse_peak`, `w.stamp_peak`, read by the viewer).
- **Overlap counted in frames.** Growth, slides, fills and swells compare overlap frames (the sum of interval lengths), with overlap involving the moved word weighted above all others, so a change cannot lengthen an existing overlap or trade one for another.
- **Hold-hide partners** (`pairHoldHides`): when the size pass hides a word for a hold, its sung neighbour that leans on it (a pronoun or filler right after it, a filler right before it) is hidden over the same frames, unless the neighbour is in its focus window or hiding it would empty the screen; then the hide is undone if that adds no overlap, else left and reported.
- **Word roles** (`word_roles.mjs`): one keyword classifier for the grey rule, the size pass and keyword mode. In order: song input override; names and numbers (including spelled numbers); pronouns; a preposition or particle ending its clause; the line-end rhyme word; a stressed word (`accent`, or written in capitals in the song map); fillers (articles, possessive determiners, prepositions, conjunctions, auxiliaries, interjections, filler adverbs, "that" as a conjunction); everything else is a content word and a keyword. Keywords are never grey (a grey keyword is made cream). Player names are never grey and are set in the jersey font. In the size pass, a keyword that cannot grow first tries hiding overlapping non-keyword fillers to make room; only if none frees room is it dropped, reported as "dropped by necessity".
- **Per-word overrides** in song settings `word_overrides`, keyed by song-map word index: `{"name": false}` (a name match that is not a name, such as a pun: no jersey font; the new font is the nearest neighbour's if it fits the jersey box width, else the narrowest that fits, else the narrowest scaled to that width), `{"score": true}`, `{"keyword": true|false}`.
- **Scores** (`score: true`): drawn as scoreboard bulb digits (viewer `scoreTexture`): each digit a seven-segment shape of bulbs on a 4 by 8 grid; lit bulbs orange-red (#FFE3B8 to #FF8A3D to #F2471C) with a wider soft glow; the unlit bulbs that would complete an 8 dim dark grey (#2E2E30). The box is sized like any word under the Type rules (cap height = bulb grid height) and takes the same entrances and motion. Not detected automatically; mark each score in the song input.
- **Round letters** (viewer): plain words are centred on their measured ink, not their advance box, and shrunk if the ink is wider than the box, so a round first or last letter (O, C, G, S, Q) is not cut flat by the texture edge.
- **Team artwork**: team wordmarks and intro badges in the song input are the teams' real artwork; the engine draws whatever image `team_wordmarks` and `intro` name.

## Engine fix brief 8 (22 November 2026)

- **Slides for words entering past a clip plane.** `slideWhole` now slides a clip word too, only across its clip planes' normals (so it still emerges from behind its host), and up to 3 of its width or height. Brief 8 item 49: IN, riding out from behind CIRCLE, had been dropped because clip words were never slid.
- **Lone holds** (`loneHolds`, reported, not failed): stretches over `LONE_HOLD` (30) frames with one readable word alone on screen, flagged when a dropped word is sung inside them. Listed in the provenance report and on the console as `LONE HOLD after a drop`.
- **Slivers** (`SLIVER_ASPECT` 0.5): a word whose drawn width, for its drawn height, is under half its true width (seen nearly edge-on, mid-flip or mid one-axis grow) does not fill the frame for the empty-screen rule. Zoom floors no longer apply to one-axis (`axis`) grow entrances.
- **Swell release** (`swellRelease`, revised in brief 10): a word swelled x2 or more does not drop straight back to its resting size. Tried in order: hold the swelled size to the end of its life; release over 12, then 8, then 6 frames to the square root of the swell, then to 1, first about the swell's own pivot and then steered about each point of a 3 by 3 grid on the word's box (a shift from (1 - k)(P_up - P) to 0 over the same frames keeps the motion continuous); finally settle at the square root over the original frames. Each is kept only with no new overlap, static partial, partly shown focused word or long empty stretch, and no loss of in-frame share; otherwise it is left and the refusals are counted in the report.
- **Sideways at the edge** (`hideUnreadable`): a word of 2 letters or fewer turned 60 to 120 degrees whose box touches or crosses the frame edge (within 2%) is hidden outside its focus window while the camera is still.
- **Fly-through** (`hideFlythrough`, revised in briefs 9 and 10): only a true pass-through. While the camera moves and the word is still, a word is hidden on a frame where the camera goes through its box between that frame and the next (or the last), or where the line of sight through the frame centre meets its box and it either reaches inside the viewer's near clip distance (`VIEW_NEAR` 20, as in viewer.html) or has letters over 90% of the frame height with under half of them in frame. Not while it must be shown whole (reported). A hide that would leave a stretch over the empty limit near-empty (a lone sideways word under 1.5 x the minimum counts as empty) is undone. It runs before the fills and again after them on the final camera; the report marks a hide that a later fill undid, and counts as visible only hides that change pixels on screen (screen area, not share of the word).
- **Largest word never grey** (`largestGreyToCream`): on any frame, the readable word with the tallest letters is made cream if grey.
- **Score panel** (viewer): bulb digits always sit on a dark scoreboard panel (#0A0F1C); the word's block colour, if any, is a thin rim around it. Unlit bulbs #24252B.
- **Wordmarks sized by their letters.** The song appearance pass sets an image wordmark's `cap` to its letter height: the fitted image height times `team_wordmarks.<name>.letter_share` (else the PNG's alpha box height for a transparent image, else 1). A wordmark-only size pass (`enforceMinSize(..., { wordmarks: true })`) then grows a small one; one that cannot grow is kept and reported. `colours.edge` draws an edge of that colour round an opaque wordmark image (viewer).
- **Static partials in candidate choice** (item 58, `build_best.mjs`): each candidate counts its focused words static and partly on screen (`staticFocusRuns`, check `13.1.21_static_partly_on_screen_focus`). It ranks candidates right after the shown-whole gate (survivors and fallbacks alike); it does not by itself fail a candidate.
- **Static partial repair after the join** (item 58, `hideStaticPartials`): a focused word static and partly on screen is slid along its plaque (up to 3 of its width or height; kept only if the word is then whole at its onset, never partly shown or slid out of frame); failing that a filler or keyword is dropped, and a name or number is hidden for its partly-on-screen span by necessity, reported.
- **Near-empty stretches** (brief 9 item 27, `SIDE_FILL` 1.5): stretches over the empty limit whose only word is turned sideways (60 to 120 degrees) with letters under 1.5 x the frame's minimum are listed in the provenance report, not failed. The song-wide empty gate and Type rule 1 are unchanged.
- **Swells stay in frame** (brief 9 item 28): a swell, and a slower swell release, is kept only if the swelled word loses no in-frame share on any frame.
- **Viewer near clip** (brief 10): `visibleFraction` clips at the viewer's near distance (`VIEW_NEAR` 20), not 1, and can return screen area (`absolute`) for the visible-pixels flag.
- **Room for a dropped keyword** (`placeInRoom`, brief 10 item 37): a keyword dropped by necessity (for size or as a static partial) is put back when room exists. Room means a placement within 3 of its widths and heights of its planned spot where the word, grown to the size minimum (with a 3% margin) and entering as a pop, is whole in frame over the shown-whole window (its appearance through its onset, at least the shown frames), overlaps no word over its life (size-drop ghosts counted) and is not a static partial at its onset. It runs once, after the static-partial hides, so a word put back never takes the spot a static partial's slide needs. The join then reruns the check on a copy; any keyword that still has room fails the join (`ROOM FOR A DROPPED KEYWORD`). The provenance report lists every necessity drop with its room result.
- **Focused hold** (brief 10): a short hold that starts after the onset no longer counts as focused when a hold containing the onset already meets the size minimum, so the word is not dropped for the later hold.
- **Real team artwork** (brief 10 item 38, Jefferson's order, replaces brief 7 item 24): the song input points at the real team files; sources are recorded in the song's `engine input/Real team artwork - sources` note. Our imitation files stay on disk, unused. This matches Type rule 4 (the team's real wordmark or logo).
