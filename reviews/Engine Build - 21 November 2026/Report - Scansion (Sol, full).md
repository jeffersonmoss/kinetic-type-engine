## Design decisions and limits

- V2 item 16 specifies per-letter lowering for syllable cascades. V3 Amendment 1 retains the whole-word reading and landing deadlines. A longer CHASING entrance missed the deadline in the fixture, so the compiler still reports the error. A whole-word entrance with a later syllable accent lets a sustained word keep its protected read.
- V2 specifies a song map and an ear-check file. The new sidecar follows the ingest ownership pattern and supplies the live scansion used by check and render. It stores stress and spelling decisions. The generated map stores the estimated times and beat flags. Existing ear-check repairs and corrections provide the effective word bounds. The proportional split assumes equal duration per pronounced vowel nucleus.
- The beat flags compare lexical stress with the measured beat grid. Sung overrides drive the accent selector. A nearby beat and a stressed syllable can disagree in a syncopated performance, and the heuristic can also guess a stress incorrectly. Verification requires listening. The import makes no additional audio analysis pass.
- The first TypeScript check caught an ES2023 array method on the ES2022 target. The replacement passed. The first CLI smoke wrapper used a Zsh reserved variable. A corrected wrapper verified the warning and direct sidecar edit. Shared memory returned `MCP tool call requires approval, but approval policy is never`. The committed history, design documents, and build reports supplied the context.
- Browser, Remotion render, and audio playback were skipped as requested. No `props/` or finished film files were edited. The successful scratch runs cleaned up. The aborted Zsh wrapper left `${TMPDIR%/}/kte-build-scansion-series-Ch9JpJ`. The directory is labeled `owner: Codex scansion build`. `safe_rm.py` reported `[Errno 1] Operation not permitted` when moving it to `~/.safe-delete/items/`.
