## 1. Score

1. **Score: 5.5 / 10.** The engine's deterministic math core (shared `wordStateAt` physics, measured font metrics, letter-anatomy anchors, pure-function camera rig, and frame-by-frame Node validation) is strong engineering that already produces high-scoring videos, which keeps it above average. ## 3. Architecture findings

### What is right (preserve these foundations)

11. **Single deterministic math core shared by Node validator and browser renderer.** Using pre-measured font metrics (`metrics.json`), SVG `textLength`, and a single `wordStateAt` function lets headless builders validate margins, overlaps, and readability frame-by-frame without launching Chrome. This is the engine's highest-leverage asset.
12. **World-board + letter-anatomy anchors + pure-function camera rig.** Anchoring new type to named glyph parts (`flankR`, `foot`, `counter`, `stemTop`) on a continuous world canvas with a pure `cameraAt(board, frame)` (`engine/camera.ts` line 16) is the exact right abstraction for Pulp Fiction typography.
13. **Single-SVG compositing with running-average smear blur and projected 3D turns.** Keeping 3D turns (`TurnProjected`, `engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 125-175) and motion blur inside one SVG preserves clip paths, draw order, and chunk carries without fragile HTML/SVG layer mixing.
14. **Extensible verb registry concept.** Allowing new motion primitives to plug into the word-state pipeline without editing core layout or stage code is the right extensibility model, once hardened against silent overrides and validator blind spots.

### What is wrong, ranked by impact

15. **Rank 1 (Impact: Effort = Critical, Quality = High, Reuse = Critical): No declarative choreography layer above raw world coordinates.** Every video currently hand-authors 4,200 to 8,100 lines of imperative TypeScript placing blocks at explicit world `(x, y)` coordinates, referencing global numeric word indices, and calculating rig keyframes by hand. Any lyric fix, tempo tweak, or 16:9-to-9:16 restage invalidates hand-placed coordinates. This is the #1 driver of build time, token burn, and vertical rebuild effort.
16. **Rank 2 (Impact: Effort = Critical, Quality = Critical, Reuse = High): Conflicting rule families and missing taste/sync validators.** Splitting validation across default rules, opt-in `sheetRules`, and opt-in `transitRules: 'energy'` pushed builders into spamming `rise` (~100 mentions in Ball, 97 in SD) to pass gates cleanly. Meanwhile, the engine's defaults and checks actively miss Jefferson's standing taste:
    16.1. Hard drop shadows default to ON (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 66, 113, 141, 165) with a fixed 6 px screen offset (line 67).
    16.2. Suno ad-lib parentheses are not stripped by default.
    16.3. No `readable-before-sung` (max 4-frame lead) check exists, causing repeated timing rework (DOGE, Paper Trail, Ball).
    16.4. No entrance-variety check or single-move share cap exists (`variety` only checks camera angle).
    16.5. `sung-cap` (56 px) does not guard all non-primary text (echoes, small accents), and film-local per-letter/ghost verbs (`snap`, `decoy`) are invisible to the validator.
    16.6. Authoring helpers ship with validator-hostile defaults: `runTrack` defaults to `lead: 12` (`engine/authoring.ts` line 123) while its own comment warns you need negative lead to preserve the first word's 20-frame margin; `stackSteps` (`engine/authoring.ts` lines 67-70) overlaps steps with word landings in a way that fights `sheetRules` (`camera-rest` / `entrance-in-move`).
17. **Rank 3 (Impact: Effort = High, Quality = Medium, Reuse = Critical): Global mutable singletons and object-identity caches.**
    17.1. Module-level `let` bindings in `configureEngine` force separate Remotion entry points, fragile import-order checklists, and duplicated song modules just to render 16:9 and 9:16 for the same song.
    17.2. `WeakMap<Board, BoardLayout>` and `boardCues` caches keyed on object identity silently ignore post-layout mutations (causing Ball H4's Midnight-on-Midnight bug).
    17.3. Global `registerVerb` silently overwrites built-in verbs for the entire bundle (DOGE B1 overwrote `sprout`).
    17.4. Load-time reads in `engine/authoring.ts` (item 3 above) cement single-format coupling.
18. **Rank 4 (Impact: Effort = High, Quality = High, Reuse = Critical): Zero automated 16:9-to-9:16 restaging.** Every vertical Short and full vertical is rewritten from scratch. Because hand-rewriting a full vertical is tedious, builders fall back to top-to-bottom vertical stacking, triggering Jefferson's "scrolling feed" rejection (Brief items 15 and 17).
19. **Rank 5 (Impact: Effort = High, Quality = Medium, Reuse = Critical): Packaging, repo hygiene, and prop entanglement.**
    19.1. 31 modified tracked files and ~40 untracked files sit uncommitted in `ENGINE/` (including `turn3d.tsx`, `transitRules.ts`, `spread.ts`, `wah.ts`), plus a detached worktree inside the repo itself, making commit pins meaningless.
    19.2. Per-video repos duplicate `package.json`, `node_modules`, `remotion.config.ts`, `tsconfig.json`, `validate.mjs`, `measure-fonts.mjs`, `metrics.json`, and `Root.tsx` composition plumbing. Relative `../../Kinetic Type Engine` aliases break `/tmp` worktrees.
    19.3. Reusable film-local verbs (`glide`, `tiltTo`, `layDown`) were copy-pasted from Ball Don't Lie into En Fuego instead of promoted into the engine.
    19.4. Props (`props/`, `engine/burst.tsx`, palette roles `body`/`detail`/`ground2`, `figureLook`, prop verbs, and prop validator rules) are tangled directly into the core engine.
20. **Rank 6 (Impact: Effort = High, Quality = High, Reuse = Medium): Song-map ingestion fragility.** Fixed input paths, glued punctuation (`ring!)`) stretching word durations to 12+ seconds, hand fixes not propagating to line start/end/beats, missing section detection, and glued chant lines cost ~20 minutes of manual cleanup per song and inject subtle timing bugs downstream.
21. **Rank 7 (Impact: Effort = High, Quality = High, Reuse = Medium): Fragmented chunk seams and manual `Full.tsx` assembly.** All-or-nothing handoff declarations block parallel work, seam fixes get forked into `Full.tsx` board copies, and outgoing carries needed multiple patches (`carriedOff` in `engine/TypeStage.tsx` line 257, `carryStillSpan`, `carryClear`) because chunks are assembled imperatively rather than compiled as one timeline.
22. **Rank 8 (Impact: Effort = Medium, Quality = High, Reuse = Medium): Renderer/validator fidelity and performance cracks.**
    22.1. `BoardView` smear pass layering drops `over` graphics and `overlays` behind `sharpBlocks` (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 259-277).
    22.2. `WordView` (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 61-63, 107) mathematically divides out a fake `tn.stand` transform injected by `wordStateAt` for the validator, which breaks down whenever another verb rotates or scales mid-turn and risks edge-on instability.
    22.3. Switching from whole-word `textLength={g.w}` (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` line 75) to per-letter `textLength={g.adv[k]}` (line 98) when `wah` or `spread` starts risks sub-pixel jumps, and `textLength` collapses padded spaces.
    22.4. Staggered per-letter 3D turns (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 134-152) multiply SVG clipPaths and `<text>` nodes up to 64x per letter, and `BoardView` recomputes `wordStateAt` for every word on every smear sample.

## 4. Proposed target architecture

### 4.1. Package layout and prop isolation

23. **Split into clean workspace packages (single shared dependency, zero relative aliases).** Convert `Kinetic Type Engine` into a versioned npm workspace (or local tarball/workspace packages installed via `file:` / workspace link, pinned by git tag):
    23.1. `@kte/core`: immutable `EngineContext`, types, metrics, layout engine, camera rig, SVG stage, smear compositor, and 3D plane projector. Zero prop references (`figureLook`, `body`, `detail`, `ground2`, `MONO_FONT`, `burst.tsx`, and prop-only verbs removed from core).
    23.2. `@kte/transitions`: the complete built-in kinetic-type transition library (entries, exits, morphs, bridges, accents, masks, camera moves) with strict non-overwriting registration.
    23.3. `@kte/choreo`: declarative storyboard-to-board compiler + responsive 16:9 / 9:16 layout solver + seam/carry coordinator.
    23.4. `@kte/validator`: frame-by-frame legibility, sync, contrast, overlap, and variety checker.
    23.5. `@kte/songmap`: audio alignment ingestion, sanitization, beat/section analysis, and ear-check report generator.
    23.6. `@kte/cli`: one-command `kte new`, `kte ingest`, `kte check`, `kte fonts`, `kte preview`, `kte render`.
    23.7. `@kte/props` (isolated optional package): houses `props/`, `burst.tsx`, prop palette extension, prop verbs (`bite`, `burn`, `smoke`, `count`, `brake`, `wipe`, `explode`), and prop validator plugin (`prop-clear`, `prop-size`, `keepClear`). Pure kinetic-type videos never import `@kte/props`.

### 4.2. Immutable `EngineContext` (no global state)

24. **Replace `configureEngine` globals with an explicit, frozen `EngineContext`.** Every engine function takes `ctx: EngineContext` (and React components receive it via `<StageProvider ctx={ctx}>`). One Remotion `Root.tsx` can render 16:9 Master, 9:16 Full Vertical, and 9:16 Short in the same bundle with zero import-order rules.

```ts
export interface FormatSpec {
  id: 'wide-16x9' | 'vertical-9x16' | 'short-9x16';
  width: number;        // 1280 (wide) or 720 (vertical)
  height: number;       // 720 (wide) or 1280 (vertical)
  renderScale: number;  // 1.5 -> 1920x1080 or 1080x1920
  safeBox: { x0: number; y0: number; x1: number; y1: number };
  maxWordsPerRow: number; // 3 (wide) or 2 (vertical)
}

export interface StyleDefaults {
  shadows: false | { offsetRatio: number }; // default: false
  stripSunoParens: boolean;                 // default: true
  minCapPx: number;                         // default: 56 (all text)
  maxEarlyFrames: number;                   // default: 4
  minRestFrames: number;                    // default: 10
  maxSingleMoveShare: number;               // default: 0.25
  minContrastRatio: number;                 // default: 4.5
}

export interface EngineContext {
  readonly song: SongMapV2;
  readonly format: FormatSpec;
  readonly fps: number;
  readonly palette: PureTypePalette; // ground, paper, accent, accent2, highlight, light[]
  readonly fonts: Record<string, FontSpec>;
  readonly metrics: FontMetricsSet;
  readonly transitions: TransitionRegistry; // frozen built-ins + video extensions (no silent override)
  readonly defaults: StyleDefaults;
  readonly bannedSpellings: RegExp[];
}
```

25. **Eliminate object-identity `WeakMap` caches.** Replace mutable `Board` + `WeakMap<Board, BoardLayout>` with an explicit pure compilation step `compileShow(ctx, show): CompiledShow` that produces immutable `CompiledBoard` objects containing pre-computed `BoardLayout`, resolved cues, and rig tables. Mutating a spec and recompiling can never serve stale colors or layouts.

### 4.3. Song-map ingestion (`@kte/songmap`)

26. **Fix all six ingestion faults in one deterministic pipeline (`kte ingest`).**
    26.1. Accept explicit `--alignment <path>`, `--audio <path>`, and `--manifest song.manifest.json` (no hardcoded `analysis/Suno lyric alignment.json`).
    26.2. **Token and line hygiene:** Split glued newline/chant tokens (`"(One! Two! Three!)\n\nFour"`) into distinct lines before alignment mapping. Strip Suno ad-lib parentheses by default (preserving `isAdlib: true` metadata on the word so echoes can be styled intentionally; allow per-line `keepParens: true` opt-in).
    26.3. **Outlier duration clamping:** Detect words stretched by glued trailing punctuation (such as `ring!)` spanning 12.5 s) using energy drop / next-onset heuristics and a configurable max word duration (`maxWordDur_s`, e.g. 1.5 s unless flagged sustained), clamping `end_s` and logging every auto-clamped token to `analysis/ear-check.md`.
    26.4. **Cascade fixes to lines and sections:** Apply `manifest.merges`, `manifest.display`, `manifest.fixes`, and `manifest.bannedSpellings` BEFORE computing `line.start_s`, `line.end_s`, `line.start_beat`, and `line.end_beat`.
    26.5. **Section detection fallback:** When Suno `[section]` tags are missing, infer coarse section breaks from librosa structural novelty / bar multiples (8 or 16 bars) or read explicit bar/time markers from `song.manifest.json`.
    26.6. **Stable word IDs:** Emit both sequential numeric index `i` and stable ID `id: "L04.W02"` plus `sectionId` in `song-map.v2.json` so fixing one merged word in Verse 1 never breaks word indices in Verse 3.

### 4.4. Declarative per-line choreography format (`@kte/choreo`)

27. **Author intent per line, not world pixels.** A storyboard maps 1:1 to a declarative `ChoreoChunk` file. Hand-authored low-level `Board` blocks remain available as an escape hatch (`kind: 'raw'`) for rare bespoke set-pieces, while 85-95% of every video is compiled from concise, readable choreography entries:

```ts
export interface ChoreoLine {
  id: string;                             // matches song-map line id, e.g. 'L04'
  bg?: PaletteRole;                       // ground colour change (triggers wipe/portal)
  hero?: string | string[];               // word text or local index -> E3 scale
  emphasis?: string[];                    // E2 words
  echoStyle?: 'small-italic' | 'whisper-margin' | 'parenthetical-accent';

  // Spatial relationship to previous on-board text (Pulp Fiction board continuity)
  relation:
    | { kind: 'from-letter'; target: WordRef; part: LetterPart; side: 'R' | 'L' | 'U' | 'D' }
    | { kind: 'fan'; angle: number }                    // +/-12 to 24 deg at joint
    | { kind: 'quarter'; dir: 'cw' | 'ccw'; deg?: 90 }  // demotes old block to margin
    | { kind: 'counter-nest'; target: WordRef; letter: string }
    | { kind: 'stack'; measure?: 'safe' | number }
    | { kind: 'replace-in-place'; target: WordRef }     // for morph / turnSwap / matchCut
    | { kind: 'run-wide' }                              // single wide tracking row
    | { kind: 'seam-rule'; side: 'R' | 'L' | 'U' | 'D'; extendFrom?: WordRef };

  // Built-in transition vocabulary (validated for variety + sync automatically)
  enter: EnterSpec;       // e.g. { type: 'turn3d', axis: 'y', pivot: 'left', stagger: 1 }
  accents?: AccentSpec[]; // e.g. [{ word: 'BOOM', type: 'beatPunch', scale: 1.3 }]
  camera?: CameraIntent;  // e.g. { mode: 'frame-line' | 'frame-hero' | 'follow' | 'orbit', tilt?: 12 }
  exit?: ExitSpec;        // e.g. { type: 'demote-sideways' | 'vanish' | 'tumble' | 'turnOut' | 'clearDisc' }

  // Optional format-specific tweaks (keeps 16:9 and 9:16 in one source)
  vertical?: Partial<Pick<ChoreoLine, 'relation' | 'enter' | 'camera' | 'hero'>>;
}
```

28. **Format-aware layout and camera solver (`compileChoreo`).**
    28.1. Walks `ChoreoChunk[]` sequentially, placing each line in world space relative to the prior line's resolved letter/block geometry, automatically resolving non-overlap against active unretired blocks and respecting `ctx.format.safeBox` and `maxWordsPerRow`.
    28.2. Automatically computes rig keyframes (`fanTurn`, `quarter`, `runTrack`, `stackSteps`, `station`, `cameraFollow`) with **conflict-free timing**: transits finish before landings or are classified cleanly under the unified transit rule; `runTrack` automatically computes safe lead; `stackSteps` spaces moves so they never collide with landings.
    28.3. **Automatic 9:16 restaging without losing energy:** When compiling for `vertical-9x16` or `short-9x16`, the solver re-wraps rows to at most 2 content words inside `safeBox` (`x: 48..612, y: 102.4..1024`), redirects long horizontal `run-wide` / `R` chains into diagonal/vertical/quarter-turn progressions (turning retired lines into crisp tall left/right spines), keeps all 3D turns, morphs, fans, and scale punches intact, and applies any `line.vertical` overrides.
    28.4. **Unified seam compilation:** Chunks declare `seamOut: { kind: 'slideUp' | 'quarterCW' | 'matchCut' | 'portal' | 'cut', atLine?: string }`. The compiler aligns outgoing and incoming boards automatically (including outgoing ground/vignette suppression and exact frame windows), eliminating manual `Full.tsx` seam patches.

### 4.5. Unified transition contract (`@kte/transitions`)

29. **One clean interface for all motion, with mandatory validator geometry.** Replace the split between `emerge`, `verb`, `turnSwap`, `block.exit`, and `board.exit` with a single `TransitionPlugin` contract where every transition returns BOTH render instructions and exact validator geometry (`visibleHull`), removing the `TypeStage.tsx` lines 61-63 `tn.stand` division hack and eliminating validator blind spots on per-letter or ghost effects:

```ts
export interface WordFrameEval {
  opacity: number;
  inkOpacity: number;              // max visible letter opacity
  hidden: boolean;
  transform: Transform2D;          // clean 2D transform (no 3D stand-in hacks)
  plane3D?: TurnPlane | TurnPlane[]; // exact 3D projection planes if turning
  clip?: ClipRegion;               // convex poly or SVG path
  letters?: LetterEval[];          // optional per-letter state
  ghosts?: GhostEval[];
  colour?: string;
  // Mandatory validator geometry in block space (exact projected hull for 3D, expanded box for spread/per-letter)
  visibleHull: Pt[];
  isInTransit: boolean;
  readingEnded?: boolean;
}
```

### 4.6. Unified rule profile and automatic checks (`@kte/validator`)

30. **Replace the three conflicting rule sets with one pure-typography profile (`KINETIC_V2`).** Drop the `sheetRules` vs `transitRules: 'energy'` toggle war. `KINETIC_V2` allows lively entrances, 3D turns, and fast camera moves (no speed cap on intentional kinetic transits), while strictly enforcing what actually governs viewer quality:
    30.1. **Sync (`readable-before-sung`, `display-late`):** Error if a word reaches >50% visible ink more than 4 frames before `word.start_s` (unless explicitly tagged `preset: true` or `relight`), or is not readable by `start_s + 1` frame.
    30.2. **Universal legibility (`min-cap`, `contrast-ratio`, `rest-read`, `safe-margin`):** Error if ANY displayed text (sung word, echo, caption) has screen cap height < 56 px, WCAG luminance contrast < 4.5 against its active ground/plate (making Midnight-on-Midnight impossible), fewer than 10 frames at rest at reading opacity, or crosses `ctx.format.safeBox` during its read window.
    30.3. **Entrance variety (`consecutive-enter`, `move-share`, `kinetic-variety`):** Error if two adjacent lines use the same entrance family; error if any single entrance family accounts for more than 25% of lines in a chunk (killing `rise` spam); warning if 3 consecutive lines share the same rest angle AND have no 3D/morph/scale-change event.
    30.4. **Exact overlap (`ink-overlap`, `anchor-origin`):** Overlap checks run on `WordFrameEval.visibleHull` (closing the per-letter and ghost blind spots). `anchor-origin` errors on dummy `{point: {x: 0, y: 0}}` anchors unless the line is the first line of a board or an intentional screen-edge `slideFrom`.
    30.5. **Glyph coverage (`missing-glyph`):** Fails immediately if any character in `song-map.v2.json` is missing from `metrics.json`, printing the exact `kte fonts measure` command.

### 4.7. Rendering fixes (`@kte/core`)

31. **Fix smear layer ordering (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 259-277).** Partition `over` graphics and verb `overlays` by their owner's pass (`blur` vs `sharp`) so sharp words and their own overlays always composite cleanly above blurred background elements.
32. **Eliminate whole-word vs per-letter SVG jump and space collapse.** When a word uses any per-letter verb (`wah`, `spread`, `cascade`, `morph`), render it with kerning-compensated per-letter positioning across its whole life (not switching DOM structure mid-hold), and preserve explicit space advances instead of collapsing them inside `textLength`.
33. **Memoize per-frame word evaluation and cap SVG 3D strip budget.** Cache `wordStateAt` per `(boardId, wordIdx, frame)` within a render pass, and support adaptive strip counts + optional WebGL/Canvas2D fallback if heavy staggered 3D turns exceed frame budgets.
34. **Versioned non-destructive render CLI (`kte render`).** Always writes to `renders/<Slug>_<format>_v<N>.mp4` (auto-incrementing `N`), never overwriting an open QuickTime file. Includes `--draft` (480p, fast x264 ultrafast, audio muxed, optional beat/safe-zone HUD overlay) for rapid motion review.

### 4.8. One-command new-video setup (`kte new`)

35. **Command:** `kte new "<Song Title>" --audio ./song.mp3 --alignment ./alignment.json --palette pulp-ink --fonts anton-dmserif-barlow`.
36. **What `kte new` automates completely:**
    36.1. Scaffolds a lean video directory referencing `@kte/*` pinned to the engine's exact git tag/commit (no copied `node_modules`, no copied `validate.mjs`/`measure-fonts.mjs`/`metrics.json`, no relative `../../` aliases).
    36.2. Runs `kte ingest` to produce `song.manifest.json`, `analysis/song-map.v2.json`, and `analysis/ear-check.md` (listing only outlier/glued/low-confidence tokens).
    36.3. Checks every lyric character against the chosen `metrics.json` and flags or measures missing glyphs immediately.
    36.4. Generates `src/video.config.ts` (palette, fonts, guard regexes, `shadows: false`, `stripSunoParens: true`) and a single `src/Root.tsx` that auto-registers `Master16x9`, `VerticalFull9x16`, `VerticalShort9x16`, and every chunk in `src/scenes/index.ts` (eliminating `register-chunk.py`).
    36.5. Generates starter `src/scenes/*.choreo.ts` stubs grouped by song section and wires `npm run check`, `npm run preview`, and `npm run render:*`.
37. **What stays hand-made:** approving `ear-check.md` / display spellings in `song.manifest.json`, choosing palette/fonts, and authoring the creative choreography in `src/scenes/*.choreo.ts` (plus any bespoke hero set-pieces).

## 5. Text transition library

38. Below is the complete kinetic-typography transition catalog for the Pulp Fiction style. ### Entries (bringing new lines/words in)

39. **Seam slide (`seam`)**: rows slide out from behind a hairline, masked at seam/prior row. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.1, 62).
40. **Sprout (`sprout`)**: scale up from 0.08 with overshoot about a named letter part. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.2).
41. **Rise (`rise`)**: word rises from behind a clipped line under its own box (or trailing edge). **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `engine/authoring.ts` lines 97-98).
42. **Slide from edge (`slideFrom`)**: cruises in from a frame edge with ease-out and optional settle drop. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `engine/authoring.ts` lines 93-94). Fix dummy `{x:0,y:0}` anchor.
43. **Directional glide (`glide`, `glideLead`, `glideLate`)**: clean directional slide with lead/ease control. **Film-local** (Ball Don't Lie and copied to En Fuego `src/drawings/shared/verbs.ts`, Brief 50.2-50.3). Promote to built-in.
44. **Arc entry (`arcIn`)**: word curves into its landing spot along a circular/Bezier arc. **Film-local** (En Fuego `src/drawings/shared/verbs.ts`, Brief 50.3). Promote to built-in.
45. **Drop and bounce (`dropBounce`, `dropIn`, `slotRise`)**: gravity-driven drop with subtle elastic settle on the beat. **Film-local** (Ball/En Fuego `src/drawings/shared/verbs.ts`, Brief 50.2-50.3). Promote and unify as `dropSettle`.
46. **Stack drop (`stack` + `stackSteps`)**: one word per row justified to a single measure, camera stepping down. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `engine/authoring.ts` lines 67-70). Fix step-vs-landing timing conflict.
47. **Typewriter (`type`)**: letters appear sequentially (staggered fade or crisp snap). **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.11). Add crisp snap-on mode without fade smear.
48. **Snap / pop cut-in (`cutIn`, `popOut`, `snap`)**: hard rhythmic appearance on percussive hits (within legibility floor). **Film-local** (En Fuego `src/drawings/shared/verbs.ts`, Brief 50.3). Promote with validator visibility.

### Words emerging from words & letter connections

49. **Drawer (`drawer`)**: new words slide out clipped behind an edge of a host word. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `engine/authoring.ts` lines 99-103, `KIT.md` 62).
50. **Shelf & extrude (`shelf`, `extrude`)**: words rise out of a hairline or a rule grown directly from a letter stroke. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.10).
51. **Nest in counter (`nest`)**: small secondary voice typed inside a letter's counter (`O`, `P`, `D`, `A`), paired with a dive. **Built-in (unused in recent 5 films)** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.3). Expose cleanly in `@kte/choreo` so it actually gets used.
52. **Door & behind (`door`, `behind`)**: old word or opaque plate swings/slides away revealing new text underneath. **Built-in (unused)** (`engine/emergence/`, `engine/authoring.ts` lines 131-132, `KIT.md` 22.4, 22.17).
53. **Hinge & fold (`hinge`, `fold`)**: new block swings in on a hairline or unfolds as old text folds shut. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.7-22.8).
54. **Shove push (`shove`)**: incoming words physically push the old block out of the way (optional corner turn). **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.6, 59).
55. **Swell repeat (`swell`)**: repeated lyric grows directly out of its earlier occurrence. **Built-in** (`engine/emergence/`, `KIT.md` 22.12).
56. **FAN joint (`attach: 'FAN'` + `fanTurn`)**: new line sprouts at 12-24 degrees from the old last letter's right flank as camera rotates through the joint. **Built-in** (`engine/authoring.ts` lines 81-85, `KIT.md` 22.16).
57. **Stem / crossbar extension (`stemExtend`, `crossbarBridge`)**: a letter's actual vertical stem or horizontal bar (e.g., `T`, `E`, `L`, `H`) elongates into a rule that becomes the shelf, seam, or underline for the next line (Pulp Fiction "hairline that changes job"). **Missing**.
58. **Insert & part (`squeezeBetween`)**: a new word rises or drops into the middle of an existing row while the flanking words slide apart to make room. **Missing**.
59. **Word catapult / ricochet (`ricochet`)**: a fast incoming word bumps an existing word at a letter edge, imparting momentum that kicks the old word off-axis or off-screen. **Missing**.

### Morphs, word swaps, and match cuts on shared letters

60. **Letter morph (`morph`, `morphMarker`, pinned suffix `match: 'end'`, `roll: true`)**: kept letters travel or hold pinned while changed letters close/open or roll horizontally. **Built-in + Uncommitted** (`engine/authoring.ts` lines 12-29; `engine/morphPin.ts` uncommitted, `KIT.md` 80).
61. **Revolving 3D sign swap (`turnSwap`)**: old block turns 90 degrees to edge-on as new block turns 90 degrees in from edge-on on the same pivot. **Uncommitted** (`engine/emergence/turn.ts`, `KIT.md` 89.1).
62. **Relight (`relight`, `relightBlend`)**: later lyric lights and pulses a word already sitting on the board. **Built-in** (`engine/authoring.ts` lines 33-36); blended variant **Film-local** (Ball, Brief 50.2).
63. **Shared-letter match cut (`matchCut`, `letterBridge`)**: camera zooms/locks onto a specific shared letter (`O`, `I`, `A`, `S`) across two lines or boards so the incoming word replaces the old board around that identical locked glyph on the beat. **Missing**.
64. **Odometer / slot-machine roller (`slotRoll`)**: vertical clipped window spins through 2 to 5 rapid words or numbers before snapping onto the sung lyric. **Missing**.
65. **True hinged split-flap (`splitFlap`)**: top half of a character/word tile folds down across a horizontal midline crease to reveal the new glyph underneath. **Partial** (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 134-152 only flips whole per-letter tiles, not hinged halves).
66. **Wipe / curtain swap (`wipeSwap`)**: a moving hairline sweeps across an existing word, rewriting old letters into new letters directly behind the moving edge in one pass. **Missing**.

### 3D turn-ins, flips, and camera orbit/dolly

## 6. Process changes that cut revision rounds

96. **Replace prose storyboards with an executable Choreo Beat Sheet.** Currently Fable writes prose storyboards, Astra reviews prose, builders hand-translate prose into thousands of lines of coordinates, and Fable gates stills. Switching to a structured per-line Choreo Beat Sheet lets Fable and Astra review pacing, lyric acting, and transition variety directly on the exact file the compiler turns into boards, cutting out an entire translation layer and most layout misunderstandings.
97. **Add a 5-minute Song-Map Ear-Check Gate before any picture work.** Run `kte ingest` first and review only the concise auto-generated `analysis/ear-check.md` (glued tokens, clamped durations, display spellings, guessed section boundaries). Locking `song.manifest.json` upfront prevents mid-build index shifts and late vocal-sync rework.
98. **Enforce a Zero-Error Automated Gate before any LLM reviewer runs.** Require `kte check` (sync lead-time <= 4 frames, universal 56 px cap floor, contrast >= 4.5, consecutive-enter variety, <= 25% single-move share, exact `visibleHull` overlap, and clean chunk seams) to exit 0 before Fable, Astra, or Gemini Neon ever inspect a chunk. Never spend reviewer turns or 10% weekly usage catching mechanical failures a 2-second Node script catches.
99. **Gate motion with sound, not stills alone.** Jefferson judges motion from video (Brief item 27), and Paper Trail shipped without anyone watching at speed with audio (Brief item 71). Every chunk and rough-cut gate should run on a fast `--draft` render (480p, audio muxed, optional beat-tick/safe-zone HUD) so timing, energy, and readability at speed are checked before high-res final renders.
100. **Cap review loops to a strict 2-gate cadence.** With props removed (which accounted for roughly 78% of Jefferson's notes on Ball Don't Lie and over 90% on En Fuego, Brief item 61) and mechanical/variety rules automated, end the 10-round rating loops (Ball v3 to v13). Run: (a) Gate 1 on the Choreo Beat Sheet (text only, fast), (b) Gate 2 on the full rough-cut video with audio (three-judge majority vote, max 1 to 2 targeted passes), then Jefferson's watch, then final render.
101. **Adopt strict engine release hygiene (no uncommitted features, no copy-pasted film verbs).** Never start a new video on a dirty engine working tree. Any new move built for a video must either be promoted into `@kte/transitions` with a validator test and tagged commit at video wrap, or discarded.
102. **Derive verticals from the Master Choreo Spec after Master sign-off.** Keep Jefferson's master-first rule (Brief item 26). Once the 16:9 master is approved, compile the 9:16 Short and full vertical from the exact same `ChoreoChunk[]`, adding only targeted `vertical` overrides for the 10-20% of lines that need custom vertical framing, followed by one motion-energy and safe-zone pass.
103. **Immutable versioned output paths.** Enforce auto-incrementing render filenames (`v1`, `v2`, ...) in `kte render` so no process ever overwrites a video Jefferson is watching in QuickTime (Brief item 28).

## 7. What specifically would make the design a 9 or 10 out of 10

104. **5-minute cold start (`kte new` to first playable draft).** Running one command from a Suno mp3 and alignment JSON produces a pinned, zero-copy project, a sanitized `song-map.v2.json` with an `ear-check.md` shortlist, verified font metrics, a multi-format `Root.tsx`, and a runnable starter build.
105. **80%+ reduction in per-video code.** A full 3-minute lyric video takes roughly 300 to 700 lines of expressive, storyboard-aligned `ChoreoChunk` code instead of 4,200 to 8,100 lines of manual world coordinates and rig math, while preserving a clean raw-board escape hatch for custom set-pieces.
106. **1 to 2 revision rounds from first rough cut to approved master.** Achieved by isolating props, baking Jefferson's standing taste into defaults (`shadows: false`, `stripSunoParens: true`, high contrast), and mechanically gating vocal sync (`<= 4f` early), universal legibility (`>= 56 px`), and entrance variety before human or panel review.
107. **True single-source multi-format output (16:9 Master + 9:16 Full + 9:16 Short in one bundle).** Pure `EngineContext` plus responsive layout compilation turns an approved 16:9 master into a genuinely kinetic, safe-zone-compliant 9:16 vertical (with rotations, 3D turns, and margin spines instead of a scrolling feed) using only 10-20% vertical overrides rather than a full rewrite.
108. **Complete, validator-native Pulp Fiction transition library (35+ built-in moves).** Every move in Section 5, including committed 3D turns, 2.5D camera orbit/dolly, shared-letter match cuts, glyph masks, split-and-rejoin, slot-machine rollers, per-letter cascades, stem extensions, beat-synced punches, and margin spine demotions, shares one `TransitionPlugin` contract and reports exact `visibleHull` geometry so the validator is never blind.
109. **100% visual-to-validator fidelity and rock-solid repo discipline.** Zero smear layer-ordering bugs (`engine/TypeStage.tsx` lines 259-277 fixed), zero `tn.stand` transform hacks (`lines 61-63` eliminated), zero whole-word/per-letter switch jitter, clean tagged engine releases with automated validator + golden-frame visual regression checks on every commit.
