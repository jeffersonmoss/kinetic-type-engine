## 3. Contradictions with v2 and check of the `preset` addition

None of the items below drop the design below 9, because each has an obvious one-line resolution during implementation, but the builder should resolve them rather than implement conflicting clauses literally:

1. **Amendment 2 item 9 contradicts v2 item 25.** Amendment 2 makes `scene.exit` required on every scene except the last (with draft fallback `fade()`), yet it does not update v2 item 25, which still states that required fields are "exactly" `from`, `enter`, reply `from`, and `old`, and that "Nothing else is ever an unfilled-field error." Treat `scene.exit` on non-final scenes as added to v2 item 25.
2. **Amendment 4 item 19 outruns item 18 and Step 10.** Item 18 says Step 7's baseline `Frames to inspect.json` involves no hull pass and records only `S`, landing, transition midpoint, and scene cut frames. Item 19 then says Step 8's `edge('B')` test puts "the crossing frames" into that inspection list. Without Step 10's hull pass, `kte check` does not detect ink crossing the frame edge; it only emits the single transition midpoint frame, which may or may not land on the crossing. In Step 7/8, either add an explicit pre-`S` entrance sample frame for `edge(...)` moves, or verify the midpoint still in Step 8 and leave ink-detected crossing frames to Step 10 (v2 item 133).
3. **Unreviewed `preset` addition (Amendment 1 item 2).** Conceptually sound. Fixing `S` at `onset - 6` when a `preset` token appears earlier fits the single `[S, E]` contract cleanly: it prevents false "too early" errors while ensuring `E` extends at least to `onset + 8` (`S + 14`). Two minor details to settle in Step 7:
   - The wording "may become readable earlier; its `S` is then fixed at onset minus 6" is slightly ambiguous if a `preset` token becomes readable *after* `onset - 6` (for example, at `onset - 2`). Implement `S = max(firstReadable, onset - 6)` (with `firstReadable <= onset + 2`) if `preset` merely relaxes the early bound, or state explicitly that `preset` requires readability by `onset - 6` at the latest.
   - Frames earlier than `onset - 6` sit outside `[S, E]` and are unchecked by hard picture rules. That is an acceptable trade-off so prior lines finishing nearby do not trigger false failures.
4. **Minor `stay()` timing detail (Amendment 1 item 7).** `dock`, `retract`, and `clear` start at `max(incomingEntranceStart, lastE + 1)`, while `stay()` dims at `lastE + 1`. Across a long instrumental gap between lines, `stay()` would dim the only text on screen before the next line starts entering. Starting `stay()` at `max(incomingEntranceStart, lastE + 1)` keeps all four fates consistent while still protecting `[S, E]`.
