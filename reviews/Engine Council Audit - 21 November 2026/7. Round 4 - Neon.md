### Two small implementation snags to fix during the build (non-blocking)

Neither of these requires a v3 revision loop, because the fixes are obvious and local, but the implementer will hit them if built literally:

- **`scoped` vs the item 17 import rule (items 17, 20, 127).** Item 20 puts `scoped(Component)` in `context/EngineScope.tsx` and says every config-reading engine component (`TypeStage`, `BoardView`, `WordView`, smear stage) is exported through `scoped`. However, item 17 forbids `engine/` from importing `context/`. If `engine/` imports `scoped` from `context/`, the Step 4 import-rule script rejects it (and creates a cycle); if only `context/` re-exports `TypeStage`, internal child renders like `WordView` stay unwrapped and Astra's render-time leak returns. Fix in Step 4: place a tiny `ScopeRunnerContext` and `scoped` helper inside `engine/` (importing only `react`), and have `context/EngineScope.tsx` supply `withEngine(ctx, fn)` into that provider.
- **`Frames to inspect.json` sequencing in Step 8 (items 130, 131.5, 132, 133).** Step 8 item 131.5 requires inspecting `Frames to inspect.json` in the browser, but Step 7's `check` does not list emitting that file, `kte sheet` is in Step 9, and the hull pass is in Step 10. Fix in Step 7: emit the baseline `Frames to inspect.json` (first readable, landing, and mid-transition frames from `compileScore`) in Step 7 so Step 8 can inspect them in Remotion Studio, then add `kte sheet` in Step 9 and hull-flagged frames in Step 10.

Fable's known slip (`stay` missing from the `hook.ts` import line, noted in item 44) is trivial and non-blocking.

## 3. Response to Fable's rulings

- **Against Neon: draft `old` fallback is `clear(fade())`, not `stay()` (item 148).** **Accept.** Fable is completely right. Using `stay()` on every unfilled line in a long scene would pile lines onto the board until layout fails, defeating the purpose of a zero-edit draft fallback. `clear(fade())` keeps drafts clean and legible while staying obviously plain.
- **Refinement of Neon's SSR fallback: React scope test moves to the host in Step 8 if SSR fails (item 149).** **Accept.** Serialised `layoutBoard` and `wordStateAt` hashes do not execute `TypeStage`, so they cannot verify React render-time scoping. Moving test (a) to the host in Step 8 is the right safeguard.
- **Other rulings and unasked change.** I also agree with Fable's rulings on compile-time draft fallbacks (item 146) and the one-line-pair legacy timing baseline (item 147). The unasked change turning quarter rolls into `turnCW`/`turnCCW` placements read by `follow()` (item 151) is strictly cleaner than an author-written `quarterRoll` camera recipe because board orientation and camera roll cannot disagree.
