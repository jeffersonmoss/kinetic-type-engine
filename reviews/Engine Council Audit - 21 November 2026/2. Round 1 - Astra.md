**Compile one storyboard into an immutable film timeline.** Every chunk preview, seam preview, validator run and final composition should select a range of that same timeline.

Here `pin: "screen"` is a proposed contract. The current morph probe measures drift **relative to the board through the same camera**, so a kept letter can pass while moving across the screen. **New-video command.** `kinetic new "Song Title" --audio song.mp3 --alignment corrected.json` should generate the film manifest, normalized map, editable storyboard seeded with the actual lyrics, shared font/style references, and one composition entry that discovers named ranges. Offer Suno acquisition when a clip ID is supplied. Reuse measured font assets by font-file hash and glyph coverage. Reference or stage the audio once, lock an immutable engine package and renderer dependencies, and make reruns resume setup without overwriting authored lines. Initialize wide, Short and full-vertical profiles; leave cut selection and variant rendering until the master and edit are approved. The hand-made work is the music decision: which word matters, what it grows from, where the eye goes, and what repeats.

**Migration order.** First capture the working engine as a reproducible release, including its untracked features, and retain the existing board API. Next ship the scaffold and compile a verse, hook and seam into existing boards. Then move configuration and caches into composition instances, verify parity, and expand the glyph evaluator where effects require it. This puts the first authoring improvement in a real film before committing to a renderer rewrite.

## 6. Process changes that cut revision rounds

1. **Settle the audio exceptions first.** Hear the glued chant, long punctuation tail, uncertain onset and every Short splice against the actual audio. Record the correction once in the song inputs. Regeneration should reproduce the accepted map without hand-patching generated JSON. [Room for One exceptions](</Users/jeffersonmoss/Experiments/Room for One Music Video/analysis/Song map summary.md:19>)
2. **Storyboard transitions in pairs of lines.** For each pair, record what remains visible, what physical part supplies the next line, which word takes emphasis, and where the eye rests on its sung frame. “The T of WHAT becomes the wall that the reply slides out from” is executable and reviewable. “Use more rotation” leaves the central decision to the builder. [Reference sequence](</Users/jeffersonmoss/Experiments/Buy This IPO Music Video/storyboard/Storyboard v3 - Pulp Fiction Motion.md:30>)
3. **Review one representative motion proof before building the song.** Include a fast verse, a hook entrance, a quiet reply, a difficult connection and a seam. Play it at speed with sound. Judge whether the type delivers the lyric and whether the eye finds the next word. Ball’s signed-off seam review explicitly records that no audio was played. [Seam review](</Users/jeffersonmoss/Experiments/Ball Don't Lie Music Video/reviews/Fable gate - Full film seams v1.md:10>)
4. **Give technical checks and taste checks different consequences.** Missing lyrics, a broken link, visible forbidden typography or a failed approved read window should block delivery. Repeated entry families should generate a short playback reel for judgment. An intentionally recurring hook can be exactly right. Show actual earliest-readable frames and specific warnings instead of asking an owner to interpret a clean error count.
5. **Review changes in context, then watch the assembled cut.** A changed line gets a clip containing its predecessor and successor, using the final camera, masks and sound. The final gate covers the actual encoded film at playback speed, including audio joins. Give each review cut a new filename and collect timestamped notes against that cut. Build the wide master first, then review the approved Short edit and portrait restaging. [Vertical workflow](</Users/jeffersonmoss/Experiments/Kinetic Type Engine/VERTICAL SHORT WORKFLOW.md:22>)

## 7. What would earn 9/10 or 10/10

**9/10:** A new song reaches a watchable, synchronized verse and hook from one setup command and an authored storyboard. That proof contains a genuinely connected word-to-word transition, a readable 3D turn and a seam. An independent clean checkout reproduces the approved result. Changing the source letter or vocal onset updates the linked shot, previews and validator together. Wide, Short and full vertical compile from shared intent, with explicit overrides for shots that need different staging.

**10/10:** Demonstrate the gains on **two contrasting finished songs**, including a dense passage and a sparse one. As proposed acceptance targets, aim for at least **80% of lines** authored without custom TypeScript, **zero film-only seam patches**, and **no more than two owner correction rounds per master**. Record setup time, authoring time per finished minute, and the causes of corrections. Jefferson’s judgment of motion with sound is the final quality measure. A film can meet every engineering target and still need a better transition.
