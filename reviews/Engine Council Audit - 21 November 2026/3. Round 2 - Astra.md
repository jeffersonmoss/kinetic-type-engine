## Recommended architecture, one page

51. **Modules.** `song` imports audio, alignment, display corrections, vocal roles, beats, and stable occurrence IDs. `score` stores editable line cards and refrain motifs. `effects` supplies typed motion and projected drawing primitives. `compile` resolves the score, edit, format, layout, camera, and seams into immutable boards and a time index. `frameAt` evaluates their ordered glyphs, masks, colours, and layers. `check` and the Remotion `host` consume that result. `cli` owns setup, preview, and rendering. The optional legacy entry point serves existing boards and props.
52. **Per-line format.** The source and successor belong in the same declaration. In this proposed example, the audience keeps its eye on `AGE` as `PAGE` becomes `STAGE`. Correcting the vocal onset should retime the landing and source retirement together.

    ```ts
    line("verse.stage", {
      sing: word("verse.stage", "STAGE", 1),
      from: glyphRun("verse.page", "PAGE", "AGE"),
      enter: morph({ keep: "AGE", pin: "screen" }),
      land: vocalOnset("verse.stage", "STAGE", 1),
      camera: holdAnchorThroughLanding(),
      leave: keepLandmark({ letter: "T", part: "foot" }),
      portrait: { dock: "upper-left" }
    })
    ```

53. **New video.** `kte new "Song Title" --audio song.mp3 --alignment alignment.json` locks the host, checks font coverage, imports the song, writes the ear-check list, seeds cards with actual lyrics, and produces a playable widescreen draft. A rerun preserves edits. The author chooses the hero words, physical connections, refrain variations, and exceptional raw shots. The compiler schedules from the sung landing backward and reports a specific conflict if the connection cannot fit.
54. **Defaults and formats.** New films use pure type, shadow-free words and plates, and display spelling before casing. Guidance parentheses stay in source metadata; the ad-lib remains visible when sung. Compose against each format’s actual safe rectangle. Approve the widescreen master first. A full portrait version reuses the performance. A Short uses an approved edit list shared by audio and picture. A separately sung radio edit gets its own alignment and explicit occurrence mapping. [Current format insets](</Users/jeffersonmoss/Experiments/Kinetic Type Engine/engine/format.ts:7>)
55. **Checks and review.** Fail on missing lyrics or glyphs, ambiguous references, invalid effect parameters, unintended shadows or parentheses, broken seams, and missed approved reading windows. Report onset, first readable frame, contiguous read time, local contrast, clipping, and occlusion by lyric occurrence. Flag repeated motion families for playback. Verify the hardest verse, hook, reply, and seam with sound before expanding the film. A timestamped note selects the relevant card and neighbouring lines. The final watch uses the encoded film.
56. **Versioning and migration.** Capture the working features in a reproducible release before relying on a pin. Lock engine source, fonts and metrics, renderer dependencies, score schema, song inputs, and audio edit. Give every review render a new filename. Prove the first compiler on one existing verse, hook, and seam against the legacy output. Require a clean checkout to reproduce the accepted frames and audio timing. Then expand the catalogue and portrait solver. The existing `stack` source/direction collision makes a good first regression case.
