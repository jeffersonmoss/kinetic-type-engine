# Amendment: block capitals by default (Jefferson, 21 November 2026)

Organized notes by the supervising Claude session. Adds to the taste defaults (v2 Section 4) and to `kte new` (Section 5). Built in step 7.

## His words
"I want to add to the engine that it should prefer block letters because they give cleaner lines to like extrude other text from, you know, or hide other text, they play a lot more nicely with the sort of kinetic typography moves that we're making... It looks better with a block text that's like all caps. That way, the text is as close to being a rectangle as possible, which makes the transformations look crisper and cleaner."

## What the Pulp Fiction reference shows (contact sheet of 20 frames, checked 21 November 2026)
1. Hero words are a heavy, wide-bodied face in capitals (FROM, YOU, AT, DON'T, BLACK), set so large they bleed off the frame and their flat tops and stems line up with the words beside them.
2. Rows are stacked with almost no leading, so a block of words reads as one rectangle; new words grow out of, slide behind, or are revealed from those flat edges.
3. Small connectives sometimes stay lower case in the original. Jefferson's direction is all caps, so this engine sets them in capitals too.

## Rules
4. Every displayed word is in capitals by default, connectives included. A lower-case word needs an explicit `case: 'mixed'` with a reason, reported by `kte check` as a warning.
5. The default display font is a heavy block face whose capitals are close to rectangles: Anton for hero and body tiers (already installed and measured); connectives use the block face's own capitals or a condensed heavy sans in capitals (Barlow Condensed Black or Bold), never a light or serif lower case. Fonts with round or decorative capitals, scripts and thin weights are not offered by `kte new`.
6. Tight setting: leading at or below 0.95 of cap height between stacked rows of one block, so stacks read as rectangles; flush left or justified rows within a block are preferred so edges are straight for extrusion, masking and match cuts.
7. Effects that extrude, mask, nest or match-cut use the glyph cap box (cap height by advance), not the ascender box, so edges line up exactly with the letter tops and baselines.
8. `kte check` warns when a reading word is set in a non-block font or in mixed case without a stated reason. Not an error, so a deliberate exception stays possible.
